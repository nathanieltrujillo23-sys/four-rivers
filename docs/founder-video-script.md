# Founder video: script and recording guide

**Length:** about 45 seconds (about 115 words at a calm pace).
**Where it goes:** the home page, above "What you'll learn." The spot is built and hidden; send the file and I'll add captions and a transcript and turn it on.

---

## The script

Read it out loud a few times first. Change any phrase that doesn't sound like you; it should sound like you talking, not reading.

> **[Open: look into the camera, relaxed]**
> Hi, I'm Nate. I'm a second-year student at the University of Florida, studying business administration with a minor in wealth management.
>
> **[Beat]**
> I built 4 Rivers because I kept seeing the same gap: people my age learn about money from social media, and they learn about faith at church, but almost no one puts the two together.
>
> **[Slightly slower]**
> The name comes from Genesis 2:10: one river flowing out of Eden that becomes four. Here, the four rivers are income, saving, investing and giving.
>
> **[Warm, direct]**
> It's free. It takes about two and a half hours. And every lesson is grounded in Scripture.
>
> **[Close]**
> Start with the introduction. I'm glad you're here.

**Word count:** about 115. If you run long, cut "I'm a second-year student at the University of Florida, studying business administration with a minor in wealth management" down to "I'm a student at the University of Florida."

### Lines to avoid (compliance)

You hold the SIE, not a Series 65/66 or CFP, so keep it educational:
- Don't say "financial advisor," "advice," "I'll help you invest," or anything about returns or results.
- Don't name firms you're applying to or hope to work for.
- "Course," "learn," "lessons" and "education" are all safe.

## Recording guide (phone is fine)

**Setup**
- **Camera:** your phone's back camera (sharper than the front), held **sideways (landscape)** at eye level, on a stack of books or a tripod. Record in 4K or 1080p at 30 fps.
- **Light:** face a window, never stand with one behind you. Late morning or early afternoon light is softest. Turn off overhead lights if they look yellow.
- **Background:** simple and real: a bookshelf, a plant, a clean wall. Stand or sit about 3 feet in front of it so it's a little blurred.
- **Sound:** a quiet room with soft things in it (rugs, curtains, a bed). A wired earbud mic clipped to your shirt sounds better than the phone across the room. Turn off fans and AC for the take.
- **Framing:** chest up, eyes about a third of the way down from the top of the frame, a little space above your head.

**Wardrobe**
- Solid colors, no small stripes or logos. A collared shirt or a plain sweater fits the professional look of the site.

**Delivery**
- Look at the lens, not the screen.
- Smile on the first line, then talk like you're explaining it to one friend across a table.
- Pause where the script says "[Beat]." Pauses are easy to trim, rushing isn't.
- Record the whole thing 5 to 8 times. The third or fourth take is usually the most natural.
- It's fine to record it in sections (one paragraph per take). I can join them with clean cuts.

**After**
- AirDrop or upload the best take or takes (the original file, not a screen recording) and send them over. Mention which take you like best.
- I'll trim it, balance the sound, add captions in English and Spanish, write the transcript for accessibility, and turn on the home-page section.
